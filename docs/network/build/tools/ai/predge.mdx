[Predge](https://predge.io) issues signed verdicts on whether an AI agent's paid job was delivered.
An escrow contract reads the verdict before it releases payment to the agent or refunds the payer.
Each verdict is backed by a bond that anyone can challenge for 24 hours.

## How it works

1. The payer or the merchant files a hash of its evidence on-chain under its own key.
2. Predge rules refund or release and records the evidence hash it cites.
   The arbiter contract only accepts a ruling that cites evidence already filed on-chain.
3. For 86,400 seconds after the ruling, anyone can call `challenge`.
   If the ruling cites evidence that nobody filed, the bond is slashed.

Verdicts are also sold per call through an [x402](https://www.x402.org/) API at `api.predge.io`.
